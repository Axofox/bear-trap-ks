Mechanics

Why it's not just "send everyone"

01

Each troop type's damage grows with the square root of how many you send. Doubling your archers doesn't double archer damage — it multiplies it by about 1.41. That's why 100% archers loses to a mix.

02

So the best split follows share ∝ (effective attack)². What moves it is the rally leader's Attack% and Lethality% per type — your troops fight with the leader's bonuses, not your own. Tier doesn't move it, because all three types scale identically with tier.

03

Three rallies beat one. The same troops split across 3 marches deal about 73% more damage than crammed into a single march — so always fill all three.

Damage = % of your best possible march
The math
damage[type] = √(troops[type] × army_min) × attack[type] ⁄ defense ⁄ 100 × skillmod
over 10 rounds · archers get skillmod 1.10 against the Bear

Maximising that at a fixed march size — Lagrange multipliers, since the shares must add to 1 — gives a closed form:

share[type] = k[type]² ⁄ Σ k²   and   best damage = √(Σ k²)
k[type] = base attack × (1 + attack%) × (1 + lethality%) × skillmod

Base attack is 1 : 3 : 4 (Inf : Cav : Arc) at every tier. Archers get ×1.1 because the Bear is infantry, and a second ×1.1 at T7+ / TG3+. With no leader bonuses that gives:

T6 → 3 / 31 / 66  ·  T7+ → 3 / 27 / 70

Both match the published Kingshot ratio calculators exactly — the second to two decimals (26.93 / 70.08 vs their 26.9 / 70.1) — which is the check that this model is right. The optimum beats the classic 10/10/80 by 4.2%.

The same formula is stated independently by Frakinator, a full battle simulator worth using if you want hero skills and troop losses modelled too. This page only does the ratio — quickly, on a phone.

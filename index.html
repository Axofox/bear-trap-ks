Mechanics

Why it's not just "send everyone"

01

Each troop type's damage grows with the square root of how many you send. Doubling your archers doesn't double archer damage — it multiplies it by about 1.41. That's why 100% archers loses to a mix.

02

So the best split follows share ∝ (effective attack)². Your Attack% and Lethality% per type are what move it — tier doesn't, because all three types scale identically with tier.

03

Three rallies beat one. The same troops split across 3 marches deal about 73% more damage than crammed into a single march — so always fill all three.

Why this beats the usual advice

Damage = % of your best possible march
The math
damage[type] = √(troops[type] × army_min) × attack[type] ⁄ defense ⁄ 100 × skillmod
over 10 rounds · archers get skillmod 1.10 against the Bear

Maximising that at a fixed march size gives a closed form:

share[type] = k[type]² ⁄ Σ k²
k[type] = base attack × (1 + attack%) × (1 + lethality%) × skillmod

Base attack is 1 : 3 : 4 (Inf : Cav : Arc) at every tier. With no personal bonuses that lands on 3 / 31 / 66 and scores 10/10/80 at 96% — an optimum 4.2% better. Both figures match the published Kingshot ratio calculators, which is the check that this model is right.

Why this math

Why it's not just "send everyone"

01

Archers hit hardest, so joiner ratios lean archer-heavy — but Bear Trap damage scales with a square root of army size, so stacking one type past a point gives diminishing returns. There's no single fixed ratio, only good defaults.

02

The your ratio readout above just shows what your current Infantry/Cavalry/Archer counts already add up to as percentages — no target involved, just where you stand right now.

03

The target ratio card checks that against a ratio and your rally capacity — the march limit the ratio is actually built for. "Send now" is capped by whichever runs out first, troops or capacity; "train next" tells you what to grow, never past that same cap.

Step 2 · Training plan at 0 / 20 / 80
Same troops, checked against the "Joiner 0/20/80" preset. Cavalry (94,201) already implies a 471,005-troop army at 20% — bigger than what Archer (119,422) supports at 80% (149,278). The 90,643 Infantry aren't part of a 0%-Infantry ratio, so they just sit out.
